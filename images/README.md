Photos of you, your work environment, awards and milestones.

## Naming

Lowercase, hyphens, descriptive. No spaces, no capitals, no `IMG_4471.jpg`.

## The badges

Your Coventry badges live on Badgr. **Don't screenshot them** — put the verification link in the `url:` field under `credentials:` in `data.js` and the badge name becomes a clickable, verifiable link. A live credential link is worth more than a picture of one.

## Before uploading

- **Resize.** Nothing wider than 1600px. A 6MB phone photo makes the page crawl on mobile.
- **Prefer `.jpg`** for photos, `.png` for screenshots and anything with text.
- **Check who else is in frame.** Colleagues in a work photo should be fine with being on a public site — crop them out if you're unsure.
- **Check what's on screen.** A laptop visible in the background of an office photo can leak a client name. Zoom in and look before you upload.
