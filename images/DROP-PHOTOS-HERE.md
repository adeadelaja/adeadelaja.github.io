## Naming is forgiving

You do not have to match these exactly. The site tries sensible variants and uses the first file it finds.

**Extension and case don't matter:** `.jpg` `.jpeg` `.png` `.webp` `.JPG` `.JPEG` `.PNG` all work.

**The number prefix is optional:** `graduation-a.jpg` works as well as `01-graduation-a.jpg`.

**Hyphen or underscore:** both fine.

**Only have one photo for a category?** Name it without the `-a` — just `01-graduation.jpg` — and it fills the first slot. The second stays a placeholder until you add `-b`.

> The `-b` slot deliberately has **no** fallback to the plain name. Otherwise a single photo would render twice side by side as if it were two different pictures.

**What actually matters is the word:** `graduation` · `startup-bootcamp` · `leadership-award` · `sap-emarsys` · `causeled-campaign`. Get the word right and the file lands. Get it wrong and you see a labelled placeholder — nothing ever breaks.

## Picking the two

They should show **different things**, not two angles on the same moment. The pair is there to give a fuller picture, not to repeat one.

| Category | Photo A — the moment | Photo B — the context |
|---|---|---|
| Graduation | You in cap and gown | With family, with friends, or the ceremony itself |
| Bootcamp | You presenting or working | The room, the team, the certificate |
| Leadership | The award | Rep work in progress, a meeting, the badges |
| SAP | You at the office | Team, desk setup, the building |
| CauseLed | You on campaign | The team, the venue, branded materials |

## Before uploading

- **Resize to 1600px wide max.** A 6MB phone photo makes the page crawl on mobile.
- **Check the background.** A laptop screen behind you in an office photo can leak a client name. Zoom in first.
- **Check who else is in frame.** Crop out anyone who hasn't agreed to be on a public site.

## Why they're placed this way

Every photo sits next to the claim it proves — graduation on the graduation row, SAP on the SAP row, the award inside the case study about changing the timetable. There is no gallery, on purpose. A photo in a gallery is decoration; a photo next to a claim is evidence.
